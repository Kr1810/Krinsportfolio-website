ABOUT_ME.txt

I design digital experiences that feel clear, thoughtful and human.

Hi, I'm Krina, a UI/UX and graphic designer based in Ahmedabad.

I started designing during my Computer Engineering degree, and since 2025 I've been turning ideas into real websites, apps and dashboards for clients and teams. My work covers the full journey: understanding users, sketching flows, designing in Figma, and getting the final result live.

AI is a big part of how I work. I use it to brainstorm, prototype and speed up the boring parts. But research, testing and the final design calls stay with me, because good design still needs a human eye.

Because of my tech background, I'm comfortable in Webflow and code too. That makes handoffs with developers smooth, and I can talk feasibility instead of just sending files.

Outside of interfaces, I also make social content, videos and branding assets. I like when a product looks and feels like one clear idea.
