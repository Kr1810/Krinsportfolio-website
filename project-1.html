Restro-A Restaurant Menu UI (Mobile)

A landing page with smart animation effects | Visually Appealing | Easy to Use | Categorized Layout

Live Link

Overview

In this project, I explored the art of creating a visually captivating and user-centric Restaurant Menu using Figma. The design focuses on delivering a seamless experience for users while ensuring the menu remains functional and aesthetically pleasing. I took care to structure the menu into clearly defined categories, making navigation intuitive and efficient for users.

A bonus addition to the project was the design of a landing page enriched with smart animation effects to enhance the user experience and provide an engaging entry point. I also crafted an original app logo to give the design a cohesive and polished look.

The experience allowed me to hone my skills in design aesthetics, layout optimization, and interactive UI elements.
