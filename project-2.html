Sign up & Login Work Flow UI/UX

Short and Sweet | Personalized | Useful | Error-free | Secure

Figma

Overview

In this project, I designed a mobile app signup flow with a focus on delivering an effortless, user-friendly, and secure onboarding experience. My goal was to create a flow that minimizes friction while engaging users and collecting necessary information effectively.

The design process emphasized efficiency, ensuring users could complete their signup quickly and without confusion. I incorporated personalization to foster user connection and error-handling measures to ensure a smooth journey. Security was a top priority, with design elements aimed at protecting user data and building trust.

This project allowed me to refine my skills in UI/UX design, process optimization, and user engagement strategies while keeping user needs at the forefront.
